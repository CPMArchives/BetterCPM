The BetterCP/M contributors offer their original software, including the core,
utilities, build tools and original code examples in the manuals, under the
[MIT License](LICENSE). Existing per-file notices and separately licensed
components govern the material they identify, including adaptations of upstream
programs. Preserve the original authors' notices.

The contributors license the original prose and original illustrations in
BetterCP/M's user, installation, utilities, programmer and architecture
manuals under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The [full legal text](LICENSES/CC-BY-4.0.txt) is included. Credit the named
authors where supplied, otherwise credit “BetterCP/M contributors”, link to
the license and identify changes. Keep supplied source and attribution notices.
Suggested credit: “BetterCP/M contributors, [manual title], BetterCP/M project,
CC BY 4.0”, with the manual's source URL.

These grants cover rights the contributors control. Embedded quotations,
historical manuals, upstream software and patches derived from it, imported
data, third-party screenshots, disk images and test fixtures retain their
respective terms. Directory placement, translation to another format and
inclusion in a source archive do not extend the grants to those works.

The candidate packager selects entries from the
[third-party notice inventory](packaging/1.0/third-party-notices.json) for
each independent download. Preserve its THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt, the complete
applicable license texts, and the matching source and build companions.
The [Montezuma catalogue record](third_party/montezuma/README.md) identifies
an unresolved permission question that must be settled before package freeze.
